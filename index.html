Battle log

    Each turn is two calls to gen.pollinations.ai: the state of the arena and the legal moves go out as one typed decision question, and the choice plus its probability come back. A plain text model stands in when the decision model returns nothing usable, and a legal random move stands in when both fail, so a duel always finishes. Nothing is simulated in advance: the page holds no hidden dice that the models did not touch.
